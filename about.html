    The Origin

    Born from a Desire for Genuine Stillness in a Busy World

    Prana Studio was founded in 2018 by master practitioner Elena Rostova. Having spent over a decade studying traditional asana in Mysore, India and contemplative sound therapies in the Himalayas, Elena recognized that modern city life urgently needed a sacred haven free from judgment, commercial pressure, and distraction.

    What started as an intimate gathering of six dedicated practitioners in a quiet second-story loft has blossomed into a cherished community sanctuary. Today, our 6,000 sq.ft space welcomes thousands of students monthly, offering over 40 weekly classes across 10 distinct yoga and meditation disciplines.

    Guiding Principles

    Our Philosophy & Mission

    We honor yoga as an expansive eight-limbed discipline of living consciously, not simply physical exercise.

    Anatomical Inclusivity

    Every skeleton is uniquely shaped. We teach functional alignment over rigid aesthetics, empowering each student to honor their bone structure and nervous system.

    Breath as the Anchor

    Without conscious breath, movement is merely gymnastics. Every posture is guided by smooth pranayama that quiets reactive thought patterns.

    Community & Belonging

    We reject elitism. Our studio welcomes practitioners of all ages, backgrounds, orientations, and physical abilities with authentic warmth and open arms.
