    Class Overview

    The Art of Breath-Synchronized Fluid Movement

    Vinyasa Flow is rooted in the Sanskrit term nyasa, meaning "to place with mindful intention." In this dynamic class, each breath corresponds to a specific movement: an inhalation expands and opens the chest, while an exhalation grounds and contracts the core. Our teachers craft intelligent progressive arcs that warm the muscular tissues safely before moving into standing balances, graceful binds, gentle backbends, and settling into cooling seated hip releases.

    Health & Mental Benefits

    Cardiovascular Stamina

    Continuous flowing transitions elevate steady heart rate without joint-pounding impact.

    Spinal Decompression

    Safe multi-planar rotation and elongation releasing lower back and sacral tension.

    Cognitive Calm & Drishti

    Focused visual gazing points train neural attention and diminish overthinking.

    Ujjayi Breath Mastery

    Ocean-sounding throat resonance that activates the vagus nerve to decrease cortisol.

    What to Bring & Sanctuary Etiquette

    • Comfortable Athletic Wear: Form-fitting or breathable apparel that allows full range of motion.
    • Hydration: Bring a refillable container. Studio provides complimentary filtered alkaline spring water.
    • Mats & Props: Manduka Pro mats and organic cork props are complimentary; you are also welcome to bring your own.
    • Arrival Window: Doors open 15 minutes before session. To preserve studio quietude, late arrivals are not permitted once practice starts.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Vinyasa Flow suitable for absolute beginners?
    Yes. While Vinyasa is dynamic, our instructors always demonstrate modifications using props and lower-intensity variations. If you prefer a slower pace to start, our Hatha Foundation class is also an ideal starting point.
    What temperature is the room kept at?
    We maintain a comfortable ambient room temperature of 75°F to 78°F (24°C–25°C), supplemented by our gentle far-infrared radiant panels that warm the muscles directly without stuffy or humid air.
    What is your cancellation policy?
    You may cancel or reschedule your reservation up to 4 hours prior to class time with zero penalty. Your credit will be automatically refunded to your member account.
