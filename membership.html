    Membership FAQs

    Is there any initiation fee or long-term lock-in contract?
    No. We operate with zero initiation fees, zero cancellation fees, and zero auto-renew traps. Our monthly memberships can be cancelled or paused right from your member dashboard with 5 days notice prior to the next billing cycle.
    How do guest passes work?
    As an Unlimited or Annual member, you receive guest passes automatically in your account dashboard. You can generate a digital guest pass or bring a friend directly to the front desk when checking in.
    Can I pause my membership if I travel or get injured?
    Yes. Monthly members may pause their membership for up to 30 days per calendar year, while Annual members may pause for up to 60 days. Just toggle your membership freeze in your member settings.
